# MacFind Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

MacFind does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- MacFind builds an index of file and folder **names**, sizes and dates on the disk you choose.
  It never reads file contents.
- The index is stored only on your Mac, inside MacFind's own container. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- MacFind makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- The `mf` command-line tool and its MCP mode only talk to the MacFind app running on the same
  Mac, through a private local socket. If you connect an AI assistant to MacFind via MCP, the
  search results you request are passed to that assistant, under that assistant's own privacy terms.

To remove all MacFind data, delete the app; its container and index are removed with it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nullifyr/macfind/issues.
